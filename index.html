About me

Systems thinker. People-first designer.

Good training doesn't start with content. It starts with understanding the process, the people, and what needs to change.

I'm a soon-to-be graduate of the Master of Science in Instructional Design and Technology program at San José State University, where I also work as a Graduate Research Assistant studying agentic AI in instructional design, human-AI collaboration, and experiential learning.

My path into learning design wasn't a straight line, and that's my advantage. I spent years in private-sector professional services translating complex technical work into clear, persuasive stories. Today I serve as an Innovation Program Manager for a small Bay Area county, where I lead technology and process initiatives that change how public servants work every day.

Across every role, the through-line has been the same: I find the friction in a process, help people understand why it needs to change, and design the learning experiences that carry them through it.

What I bring

Three disciplines, one approach

Training works best when it's part of a bigger picture. My experience spans the full arc, from diagnosing the problem to supporting people long after launch.

01

Process Improvement

I map workflows, surface bottlenecks, and redesign processes so they're simpler to follow and easier to teach. Often the best training fix is a better process.

02

Change Management

I've guided teams through reorganizations and new system rollouts, building the communication, buy-in, and support structures that help people adopt new ways of working.

03

Training Design

I design evidence-based eLearning, job aids, and onboarding grounded in learning theory and human performance improvement, built to be accessible to every learner.
